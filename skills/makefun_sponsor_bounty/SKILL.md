# Sponsor a make.fun bounty

## Check the deadline math before you fund

- Read the entrants counting deadline and the bounty entries open time from the composed proposal before you deposit anything.

- Refuse to fund a bounty whose entries open after its entrants counting deadline, because no entrant can count in time.

- Tell the user both times in WIB and the gap between them before you ask for a deposit.

- Answer a proposal review inside its stated window, because an unreviewed proposal can expire and refund the deposit.

- Keep the linked personal wallet funded in advance, so a live template bounty can be funded at once instead of waiting for a top up.

## Prefer a house template over a custom brief

- Use a house template when the entrants deadline is near, because a template bounty lists instantly on funding.

- Treat a custom brief as a slow path, because its house review lead is about 72 hours.

- Never fund a custom brief for an entrants deadline that falls inside that review lead.

- Check that the template schedule fits the platform run cap before you fund it, because a 7 day dataset can push a final past the 168 hour cap.

- Prefer a forward 24 hour template when a longer window would break the cap.

## Count the sponsor economics before you deposit

- Tell the user plainly that a sponsor gets Rp0 back, because the pot plus the 15 percent house fee is the cost of buying entrants.

- Count the minimum pot of 2 dollars plus the house fee plus about 0.0056 SOL rent before you propose a bounty.

- Never present entry fees as a return to the sponsor, because entry fees go into the prize pot.

- Never enter your own bounty as a participant, because a sponsor cannot enter it.

- Check the open bounty limit and the daily proposal limit before you draft, because the platform allows at most 3 open bounties and at most 3 proposals per day.

## Fund from the linked personal wallet only

- Fund a sponsor bounty only from the personal Solana wallet that is SIWS linked to the make.fun account.

- Never fund a sponsor bounty from the agent wallet, because sponsor proposals can only be funded from a linked personal wallet.

- Ask the user to sign the deposit on their phone, because the deposit signing needs the personal wallet and its keypair stays with the user.

- Verify the linked wallet holds the pot in USDC plus the rent in SOL before you ask for that signature.

## Track the bounty after it lists

- Read the listed bounty back after funding and record its entries open time, its counting deadline and its closing time.

- Report the entrant count and the board rank against the prize zone, because a bounty outside the prize zone earns nothing.

- Prefer a light entry format when entrants count is the goal, because light one liner bounties drew far more entrants than heavy formats in this window.

- Stop proposing new bounties for the same entrants deadline once the open limit or the daily proposal limit is reached.

## Available connectors

- Use `muse.connector_search` to find other connectors and `muse.skill_search` to find related skills.

- If nothing here fits, look on the web for a maintained API or MCP server and set it up with [Custom Connectors and MCPs](/opt/muse/skills/custom-connector-setup/SKILL.md), or build a custom connector.

## Websites

- [make.fun](https://make.fun/) for reading the bounty proposal, the entries open time, the entrants counting deadline and the listed bounty. Carry the proposal check result into the browser task and verify the listed times there.

## Alternatives for better results

- Enter early with a funded linked wallet instead of starting a custom brief cold when an entrants deadline is close.

- Choose a house template with a short entry format when the goal is entrant count rather than a complex judged entry.

- Skip a new sponsor bounty when the current bounty already sits outside its prize zone and the deadline math leaves no harvest time.
